How it works

Four steps, no signup

The whole pipeline runs locally against a curated knowledge base. Nothing you paste is stored or sent anywhere.

01

Parse

Resume text is normalised and scanned against 97 canonical skills. Section context matters — a skill under Internship counts for more than one on a reading list.

02

Rank

A TF-IDF content model scores semantic fit, a weighted readiness model scores skill coverage, and market demand breaks the tie. The three blend into one match score.

03

Prioritise

Every missing skill is scored by importance × how far behind you are, then labelled critical, high or medium. You see the blocking gaps first.

04

Plan

Gaps become a phased roadmap in weeks, months or years, with curated free resources and portfolio projects chosen specifically to close them.
